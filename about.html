About the Research Blog

Our Mission

Project Mars is dedicated to making scientific research accessible to students. Our blog provides high-quality, student-friendly content across multiple scientific disciplines, from pediatrics to artificial intelligence in medicine.

Our Team

Sarah Verma

Founder

Sarah founded Project Mars with the vision of creating a platform where students can access high-quality, accessible research content and develop their scientific communication skills.

Anushka Pandey

Director of Content

Anushka leads the blog's content strategy, oversees article development, and manages our team of student writers. She ensures all content meets our standards for accuracy and accessibility.

What We Offer

  • 📚 Research Skills 101 - Practical guides on how to do research
  • 🔬 Topic Categories - In-depth articles organized by scientific field
  • 👥 Spotlights - Interviews with researchers, mentors, and student ambassadors
  • 💡 Breakthroughs - Accessible explainers of major research advances

Get Involved

Interested in writing for us or collaborating? We're always looking for passionate student writers and researchers!
